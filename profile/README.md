**SHELF** (Spawning Habitat & Early-Life Linkages to Fisheries) is a
long-term monitoring program on the US Gulf continental shelf, based at the
University of South Florida College of Marine Science. We collect fish eggs
at sea with a Continuous Underway Fish Egg Sampler (CUFES) and identify them
to species with DNA barcoding, to map where and when fishes spawn.

### Standard Operating Procedures

The SHELF SOPs cover the program overview, field collection, lab methods,
and data management.

- **Read them:** <https://shelf-usf.github.io/SHELF-SOP/> (Word version
  available from the site)
- **Suggest a change:** open an issue in
  [SHELF-SOP](https://github.com/SHELF-USF/SHELF-SOP/issues/new/choose).
  A free GitHub account is all you need.
- **Field and lab protocols on protocols.io:**
  [SOP 2, Field](https://doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.wov1yj1wpvr2/v1) ·
  [SOP 3, Lab](https://doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.q2lydz4rmlx9/v1)
